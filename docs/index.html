Features

Everything a dictionary should do, and nothing it shouldn't.

Fully offline

The whole dictionary is bundled inside the app. Look up any word on a plane, on the metro or with data switched off.

Instant search

Results appear as you type. The database is built for speed, so the list stays snappy even on modest phones.

Typo-tolerant

Misspelled something? Fixionary still finds the word and suggests the closest matches.

Search by meaning

Can't remember the word? Describe what it means and let the dictionary find it for you.

Meanings & thesaurus

Definitions by part of speech, plus synonyms and antonyms, all on one clean page.

Pronunciation

IPA transcriptions and pronunciation help so you know how a word is said, not just what it means.

Origins & word forms

See where a word comes from, and find the base word behind plurals, tenses and other forms.

Favourites & history

Save the words you love and jump back to anything you looked up before.

Private by design

No ads, no trackers, no accounts and no permissions. Your searches never leave your phone.
